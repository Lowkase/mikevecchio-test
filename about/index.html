About Mike

Get to know Mike.

Mike's interest in massage therapy started after two motor vehicle accidents. Getting back to feeling like himself took a combination of massage therapy, physiotherapy and other care — and that experience stuck with him. Massage has been part of his life, physically and mentally, ever since.

He went on to study at Westervelt College's Massage Therapy program, graduating with honours in 2019, and has been treating clients in St. Thomas since. He works with people of all ages: easing everyday tension, supporting active people and athletes, helping with recovery, and simply giving people a chance to slow down and unwind.

Outside the treatment room

Mike is a husband, father of five and grandfather, so he understands better than most how busy and overwhelming life can get — and how much it matters to take a bit of time for yourself. When he's not treating clients, he's usually gardening, fishing, or out on a hike with his wife.

What Registered Massage Therapy means

"The practice of Massage Therapy is the assessment of the soft tissue and joints of the body and the treatment and prevention of physical dysfunction and pain of the soft tissue and joints by manipulation to develop, maintain, rehabilitate or augment physical function, or relieve pain." — Massage Therapy Act, 1991

Mike works with your level of comfort throughout every treatment, and a session can be adjusted or stopped at any time. Learn more about the profession at cmto.com, the College of Massage Therapists of Ontario.
