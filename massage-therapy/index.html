What a session looks like

Mike begins with a brief consultation about your concerns, treatment goals, and preferences. He then develops an appropriate plan using one or more techniques, including:

  • Swedish massage
  • Remedial exercises
  • Friction and trigger point therapy
  • Sports massage

The treatment can be adjusted or stopped at any time. Mike works at your level of comfort throughout.
