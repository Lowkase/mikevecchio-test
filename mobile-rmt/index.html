Mobile RMT

Registered Massage Therapy at Your Location

Mike brings a professional portable treatment table, linens, and everything your session needs. You just need a bit of clear floor space. A living room or bedroom both work well.

Why clients choose mobile

  • No drive and no waiting room, before or after your appointment
  • Couples and families can book up to four treatments back-to-back
  • A practical option when getting to an appointment is difficult

How it works

  • Call or text Mike with your location, preferred time, and number of people
  • Mike confirms the details, including the total cost and any travel fee
  • Mike sets up, provides your treatment, and packs up when you're done
