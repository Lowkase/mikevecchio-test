Meet Mike

Meet Mike Vecchio, RMT

Mike's experience receiving massage therapy and physiotherapy after two motor vehicle accidents influenced his decision to become an RMT. He graduated with honours from Westervelt College's Massage Therapy program in 2019 and now operates his practice in St. Thomas.

He's also a husband, father of five and grandfather, so he understands how busy life gets. Outside the treatment room you'll usually find him gardening, fishing or out on a hike.
