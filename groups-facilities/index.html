How it works

Booking for your group or facility

  • Call or text Mike with your general location, preferred date, and approximate headcount
  • Mike confirms the schedule, including how the time is divided between clients and any travel fee
  • Mike travels to your location once and treats everyone back-to-back
  • Every client gets the same one-on-one Registered Massage Therapy treatment
