About Mike

About Mike Vecchio, RMT

Mike's experience receiving massage therapy and physiotherapy after two motor vehicle accidents influenced his decision to become a Registered Massage Therapist.

He graduated with honours from Westervelt College's Massage Therapy program in 2019 and now operates his practice in St. Thomas. He works with adults of different ages and activity levels, with treatment tailored to each client's needs and goals.

Outside the treatment room

As a husband, father of five, and grandfather, Mike understands how difficult it can be to make time for yourself. Outside the practice, he enjoys gardening, fishing, and hiking with his wife.

What Registered Massage Therapy means

Registered Massage Therapy involves assessing and treating the body's soft tissues and joints to support physical function and help relieve pain or discomfort.

Mike works with your level of comfort throughout every treatment, and a session can be adjusted or stopped at any time. Learn more about the profession at cmto.com, the College of Massage Therapists of Ontario.
