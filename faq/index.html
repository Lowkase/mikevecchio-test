Appointments, Pricing, and Mobile RMT

Mike provides a receipt after each appointment. Check with your insurance provider to confirm your coverage requirements.

Mike begins with a brief consultation about your concerns, treatment goals, and preferences. He then develops an appropriate plan for the appointment.

Mike sees clients from his home-based practice in St. Thomas, Ontario. Full address details are shared when you book.

Mobile appointments are available in St. Thomas, surrounding areas, and London, including White Oaks. If you're just outside that area, get in touch. Mike reviews requests individually.

Availability and any additional travel fee depend on the address and are confirmed before booking.

Mike brings a professional portable treatment table and the supplies he needs for your session. All you need is a bit of clear space to set up.

A 60-minute Mobile RMT visit is $150, plus HST.

Yes. Mike can treat multiple people back-to-back at the same address. The first 60-minute treatment is $150 and each additional 60-minute treatment is $130, plus HST.

Mike only has to travel and set up once, so he can pass some of that saved time along when he's treating more than one person at the same address.

Mike is currently offering 60-minute mobile appointments. If you have a specific need, contact Mike to discuss it.

Mike is open to arranging visits for groups at retirement residences, workplaces and similar locations. Reach out to talk through what you have in mind.

Call or text Mike at 519-859-1419. He will confirm the details, including any travel fee, before your visit is booked.
