Mobile RMT

Massage therapy. At your place.

Mike brings professional Registered Massage Therapy directly to you — your own room, your own space, no drive there or back.

  • Available in St. Thomas, South London and White Oaks, with surrounding areas by request
  • Mike brings a professional portable table and the supplies your treatment needs
  • Just need a bit of clear floor space to set up
Booking for two or more? Mike can treat multiple people back-to-back at the same location. The first 60-minute treatment is $150 and each additional 60-minute treatment is $130, including HST.

Travel within Mike's core service area is included. An additional travel fee may apply to locations farther away — the total is always confirmed before booking.

Meet Mike

A local RMT who's been where you are.

Mike's own experience with massage therapy — after two motor vehicle accidents — is part of what led him to become an RMT. He graduated with honours from Westervelt College's Massage Therapy program in 2019, and has been treating clients in St. Thomas ever since.

He's also a husband, father of five and grandfather, so he understands how busy life gets. Outside the treatment room you'll usually find him gardening, fishing or out on a hike.
