What a session looks like

After a short conversation about what's going on and what you're hoping to get out of the appointment, Mike puts together a treatment plan using one or more techniques, including:

  • Swedish massage
  • Remedial exercises
  • Friction and trigger point therapy
  • Sports massage

The treatment can be adjusted or stopped at any time — Mike works at your level of comfort throughout.

How Mike can help

Grounded, responsible care.

Massage therapy can support how you feel and move, but it isn't a cure-all. Mike will let you know honestly what treatment can and can't do for your specific situation.

Mobility & flexibility

Sports & activity

Support for active people and athletes, before or after training.

Recovery

Stress & relaxation

Time to slow down and let your body and mind unwind.

Healthy aging

Gentler, tailored treatment for seniors focused on comfort and circulation.
