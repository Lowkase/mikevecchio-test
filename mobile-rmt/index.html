What is Mobile RMT?

Mobile RMT is Registered Massage Therapy provided in your home. Mike sets up a professional portable treatment table, provides the scheduled treatment, and packs up the equipment when the appointment is complete.

Mobile RMT may suit busy households, people who find travel difficult, and those who prefer receiving treatment in their own home.

Service area

Where Mike travels for Mobile RMT

Mobile appointments are available in St. Thomas, surrounding areas, and London.

  • St. Thomas: Mike's home base and primary mobile service area.
  • London, including White Oaks and other neighbourhoods; travel details depend on the address.
  • Surrounding areas may be available depending on travel distance. Contact Mike to confirm availability.

Availability and any additional travel fee depend on the address and are confirmed before booking.

How an appointment works

  • Call or text Mike with your general location, preferred time, and number of people
  • Mike confirms your appointment, including the total cost and any travel fee
