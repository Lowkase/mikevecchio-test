What is Mobile RMT?

Mobile RMT is the same Registered Massage Therapy Mike provides at his St. Thomas practice — just brought to your home instead. He sets up a professional portable treatment table in your space, works through your session, and packs up when he's done. No drive there, no drive back.

It's a good fit for busy households, people who find getting to appointments difficult, and anyone who'd simply rather be treated at home.

Service area

Where Mike travels for Mobile RMT

Mobile appointments are available in St. Thomas, surrounding areas, and London.

  • St. Thomas — Mike's home base and primary mobile service area.
  • London, including White Oaks and other neighbourhoods; travel details depend on the address.
  • Nearby communities depending on travel distance — get in touch and Mike will let you know.

Travel within Mike's core service area is included in the price. An additional travel fee may apply depending on the address and travel time. The total is always confirmed before booking.

What Mike brings

Everything needed for your treatment.

Mike brings a professional portable treatment table, linens and the supplies your session needs. You just need a bit of clear floor space — a living room or bedroom both work well.

How an appointment works

  • Request an appointment online or by phone, with your general location, preferred time and number of people
  • Mike confirms your appointment, including the total cost and any travel fee
  • He arrives, sets up in a few minutes, and treats you in your own space
  • Table and supplies are packed up when the session is done
