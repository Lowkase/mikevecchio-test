Groups & Facilities

Massage therapy for your residents, team, or group

Mobile RMT scaled up: Mike travels to one location and treats everyone back-to-back, so your group coordinates one visit instead of several appointments.

Who it's for

  • Retirement residences
  • Senior communities
  • Workplaces
  • Families & groups

How it works

  • Call or text Mike with your location, preferred date, and approximate headcount
  • Mike confirms the schedule, how the time is divided between clients, and any travel fee
  • Mike travels once and gives each person a one-on-one RMT treatment, back-to-back
